Companion Mouse Steering Toggle
Press G while driving to add/remove Mouse X steering with Wheel (direct) filter.
Other bindings, including keyboard steering and camera controls, are preserved.
The mouse binding state is saved through BeamNG Controls, so it persists.
The custom action is listed under Controls > Vehicle and can be rebound.
No vehicle ZIPs or original game files are modified.
Toggle OFF before disabling/removing the mod if you want mouse steering unbound.
